# KontorKaffe 3.0
Mobile portrait has a compact 3×3 customer menu, paged orders/members, five admin tabs and paged settings. Extreme short screens, keyboard, accessibility zoom and lengthy text retain natural scrolling instead of clipping. Nine existing drinks can be marked unavailable/available without replacing old orders. Menu mutation is admin-only and validated atomically at order creation. The watch uses the established service-only kk_action RPC to avoid dependency on separate REST schema exposure. Exact phone failure still needs real device confirmation. No FCM, no new accounts. Existing QR tokens and credit records unchanged.
